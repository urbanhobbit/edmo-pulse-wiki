## [2026-10-01] New Issue
- New issue: **War in Ukraine: AI-powered disinformation targets mobilization**
- Date: October 1, 2026
- URL: https://edmo.eu/publications/war-in-ukraine-ai-powered-disinformation-targets-mobilization/
- Total issues: 23
- Sections: ON THE RISE, ZOOM-IN, ELECTION BEAT, GLOBAL PULSE, ON A DIFFERENT NOTE (all 5)
- Note: Russia's war on Ukraine as the EU's main disinformation target (alongside migration). ON THE RISE: a new September wave of pro-Russian AI-generated short videos (5-15s, credible but not true deepfakes) targeting Ukrainian mobilization - soldiers in handcuffs on buses being forcibly taken away - timed with Ukrainian gains in the Donbas (Operation Vivaldi, towns north of Lyman) and reports of a possible new Russian mobilisation; EDMO had already flagged a late-2025 AI video wave around the Pokrovsk offensive (surrendering/crying soldiers). The campaign recirculates videos across European countries with multi-language subtitles and also reuses real footage (Kyiv tree-felling protest) with false captions; the editorial warns the operations are still low-quality and loosely coordinated, so the room for technical/tactical improvement is enormous. ZOOM-IN: NORDIS/TjekDet + Code for Africa on 33 recruitment groups running ~300 websites/accounts using paid Facebook ads (often AI-generated) to lure men from Africa, the Middle East, Asia and Latin America to Russia with fake $3,000+ jobs, then press-ganging them into military contracts (one in five identified African recruits killed) - ironically hosted on Western infrastructure (Amsterdam server for 'IRC Russian Bear', US/Swedish AI tools, Austrian software collecting applicant passport data); FACT debunks 'mass exodus' claims from Kyiv (metro rush-hour clip, a China toll-station photo passed off as a Kyiv traffic jam, a 2022 Poland-border image, genuine Kyiv traffic misrepresented) as panic-mongering with no evidence of mass departure; FACT on AI chatbots (ChatGPT/Gemini/Grok) cited as 'undoubtedly' identifying the Neptun Deep sea drone as Ukrainian - identical photos + neutral questions yielded three different answers (British, Russian, Ukrainian MAGURA V5), showing how AI outputs gain false authority. ELECTION BEAT: NORDIS/Kallkritikbyran - Sweden's aftermath (voter-fraud, AI 'ballots in river' fabrication, 'globalist elite' and anti-immigrant conspiracy theories), with no evidence of large-scale coordinated foreign interference and much misinformation originating domestically; GADMO/AFP - a staged Berlin video of AfD postal ballots being destroyed while CDU votes were preserved (fake, per the Berlin election authority) part of a wider autumn-2026 postal-vote false-claim pattern (Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Saxony-Anhalt) with abnormally high view-to-engagement ratios and accounts tied to pro-Russian networks; EDMO - Serbia's Vucic calls snap elections for 25 October 2026 despite earlier tying an early vote to the international security situation, which Istinomer calls inconsistent. GLOBAL PULSE: CEDMO/Demagog.pl on who creates climate disinformation (fossil-fuel interests, political actors, think tanks, media, influencers, bots, trolls; Exxon/Shell documents; Russia amplifying local tensions, e.g. after the Valencia floods); DE FACTO/Les Surligneurs on whether solar panels raise temperatures (localised +5.2C near rooftop panels in extreme heat, 3-4C nighttime warming over solar farms, but cooling when replacing asphalt; not to be confused with global climate impact - PV still far lower-carbon than fossil fuels); GADMO on a sharepic citing 10-year-old German 'development aid to China' figures (the EUR 630m date from 2017 and were mostly repayable loans; Germany has ended classic bilateral cooperation; ~368 building + 803 planned coal plants also a decade old, though China added 94.5 GW of coal in 2024 and solar passed coal in installed capacity by July 2026). ON A DIFFERENT NOTE: IPIE 'Trends in the Information Environment: 2026 Expert Survey Results' - lack of platform accountability is the top threat (78% big/extreme), ahead of misinformation (73%), polarization (69%), platform dominance (64%) and echo chambers (53%), with generative AI lowest at 47%; research funding (67%) has overtaken data access (60%) as the top barrier, and pressure to align research with government agendas rose 21%->33%, government censorship 11%->23%, company pressure 8%->18%.

GLOBAL PULSE: IDMO/Facta on Dubai influencers downplaying Iranian attacks (messaging mirroring official UAE communications); BROD on a false 'no gas this winter' claim (EU phase-out is gradual, contracts run to autumn 2027; risk is price volatility, not cold homes); CEDMO on Russian Matryoshka/Operation Overload deepfakes of celebrities (Hannigan, Parker, Roberts) with fake CNN branding ahead of the US midterms. ON A DIFFERENT NOTE: International AI Safety Report 2026 - 'loss of control' scenarios are still impossible but models increasingly distinguish test settings from deployment and find evaluation loopholes.

## [2026-09-17] New Issue
- New issue: **Fake doctors, real scams: health disinformation exploits desperation**
- Date: September 17, 2026
- URL: https://edmo.eu/publications/fake-doctors-real-scams-health-disinformation-exploits-desperation/
- Total issues: 21
- Sections: ON THE RISE, ZOOM-IN, ELECTION BEAT, GLOBAL PULSE, ON A DIFFERENT NOTE (all 5)
- Note: Health disinformation as an industry of false hope (7% of detected EU disinformation in July and August; Covid-19 separately 2%/4%). ON THE RISE: miracle-cure disinformation is the most despicable subcategory - AI-generated fake doctors and experts, fabricated studies and monetised viral scams prey on the desperation of seriously ill people; context includes sunscreen-causes-cancer claims, Hantavirus fearmongering, the 'mammography causes cancer' evergreen and Trump-boosted vaccine-autism claims; the editorial blames the consistent failure to shield people from scams and false content, noting that regulating US-based platforms is now politically and economically harder than ever for the EU. ZOOM-IN: NORDIS/TjekDet on 1,000+ AI-generated 'doctors' on YouTube producing ~100,000 misleading videos in 30 languages (e.g. fictional 'Sofie Larsen', monetisation via subscriptions/donations/supplements, abuse of hospital brands; warning signs = no uncertainty, emotional language, attacks on critics, cherry-picked evidence); BENEDMO debunks TikTok magnesium-for-ADHD claims (association not causation, inconsistent studies, weak supplement evidence); CEDMO fact-checks viral mebendazole-as-cancer-cure posts (2021 Johns Hopkins patent proves no efficacy; human trials lacking; self-medication risks interactions, liver problems, anaemia). ELECTION BEAT: NORDIS/Kallkritikbyran - Jan Teorell and John Hogstrom find no deliberate voter fraud in Sweden despite viral missing-ballot videos (replenishment delays, voters removing rival ballots; 2022 study: ~40% of officials saw ballot-paper sabotage) and dismiss the recurring claim that Election Authority head Anna Nyqvist is a Social Democrat; Sweden Democrats in Atvidaberg vs a polling site over political clothing. BECID - Latvia (Oct 3 election): four parties fight over the collapsed 'Harmony' Russian-speaking vote - 'Harmony Center' (Usakovs legacy, betrayal claims), 'For Stability!' (Ukraine aid as foreign entanglement), Stepanenko's 'Sovereign Power' (~11.8%), and Slesers's LPV. GADMO - AfD's 51.2% ballot-box vs 24.3% postal drop in Saxony-Anhalt used as 'fraud' proof: figures accurate, fraud unproven (same pattern in 2025 Bundestag: 25.3% vs 13.2%; Simon Franzmann: voter demographics + AfD telling supporters to vote in person); staged video of destroyed ballots. GLOBAL PULSE: EDMO IRELAND/FactCheck on four Trump claims in Ireland (Ireland "fleecing" the US, Irish investment doubling, Ukraine war as primary fuel-shortage cause, $5,000 per adult) - facts mixed with misleading context or unsupported; IDMO/Facta on the conspiracy-driven civil war inside MAGA after Charlie Kirk's assassination (Candace Owens amplifying claims about Erika Kirk's succession); IDMO/Facta on Iran's AI information war (fictional English-speaking female soldiers, near-identical accounts, partly unlabelled). ON A DIFFERENT NOTE: MIT's 'Your Brain on ChatGPT: Accumulation of Cognitive Debt when Using an AI Assistant for Essay Writing Task' on cognitive debt.

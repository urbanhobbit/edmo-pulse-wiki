## ON THE RISE — Climate Change Denialism, and How to Shoot the Messenger to Ignore the Message

The international scientific community is nearly unanimous in its view that climate change is real and driven by human activity. Yet every time extreme weather strikes, climate misinformation rapidly resurfaces online. The record-breaking heatwave hitting Europe is no exception, with familiar denialist claims that "it has always been hot" and "particularly hot summers have always occurred," amplified by various political figures.

A corollary of this narrative is the "chromatic change conspiracy theory" — claims that meteorological maps shaded in red to indicate high temperatures are deliberately designed to induce panic. In Austria, Germany and Ireland these claims have been debunked. An investigation by Maldita published on EDMO in 2022 found the same false narratives across much of Europe.

Despite this, Eurobarometer data shows 85% of Europeans consider climate change a serious problem, 81% support EU climate neutrality by 2050, and 88% support expanding renewable energy. These figures are recovering after a post-COVID dip. MAGA-style climate propaganda appears to have limited impact in Europe.

## ZOOM-IN

### Humans Have an Impact on Climate Change – Science Has Not Disproved This

From CEDMO. The overwhelming scientific consensus on anthropogenic global warming is based on thousands of peer-reviewed studies and endorsed by the IPCC, NASA, and other leading institutions. The article focuses on recent climate denialism in Poland, steered through selective use of expert opinions and false balance fallacies amplified by political actors, including statements by Law and Justice Party leader Jarosław Kaczyński.

### Misleading Claims About the Ecological Footprint of Solar Energy

From GADMO. Widely shared claims that solar power is not environmentally friendly because manufacturing PV systems requires energy-intensive materials omit essential context. Experts from UBA, OFI, and Fraunhofer ISE note that life-cycle emissions of solar electricity are 20-50g CO₂eq/kWh versus 400-500g for natural gas and substantially more for coal. Solar panels recover their manufacturing energy within ~1.3 years while lasting 25-30 years, and 85-95% of their material mass is recyclable.

### Is It True That Air Conditioners Only Heat City Air At Night?

From DE FACTO. Studies of urban heat islands compare both daytime and nighttime conditions and consistently find air conditioner warming effects are present both day and night — more pronounced at night due to a shallower atmospheric boundary layer trapping heat closer to the ground. Scientists focus on nighttime because warm nights pose serious health risks.

## ELECTION BEAT

### UK Helped Detect Russian Tycoon's $107M Crypto War Chest Linked to Moldovan Election Interference

From FACT. A joint Moldovan-British operation uncovered a $107 million cryptocurrency network allegedly used by pro-Russian oligarch Ilan Shor to finance election interference ahead of Moldova's 2025 parliamentary election. British financial crime specialists traced suspicious crypto flows linked to wallets connected to the Kremlin-backed stablecoin A7A5. ## GLOBAL PULSE

### Is it true that the court in The Hague recognized Russia's sovereignty over Crimea?

From BECID. In June 2026, Russia's Foreign Ministry and state-aligned media claimed a Hague arbitration court had officially recognized Crimea as part of Russia. In reality, the case stemmed from a 2016 Ukrainian lawsuit under UNCLOS concerning Russian actions in the Black Sea and Kerch Strait. The tribunal explicitly stated Crimea's sovereignty fell outside its jurisdiction and was never ruled on.

### Have 250,000 girls been sexually abused by immigrant gangs in Britain?

From NORDIS. A study by English right-wing politician Rupert Lowe cites up to 250,000 victims of "grooming gangs." The number originated with Lord Malcolm Pearson in a 2018 debate, extrapolated from abuse cases in three cities. Those local figures counted the broader category of "Child Sexual Exploitation," not specifically grooming gang victims. Fact-checkers criticize applying hotspot-city data nationally as statistically unreliable. British authorities admit the true scale remains unknown.

### Is it true that 85% of refugees are Muslims and that Muslim countries do not accept them?

From BECID. Both assertions are false. UNHCR does not collect data on refugees' religious affiliation. Many refugees originate from Ukraine and Venezuela (predominantly Christian). Several of the world's largest refugee-hosting countries — including Turkey, Iran and Pakistan — are Muslim-majority states hosting millions.

### New AI Video Generator based on Google Gemini can easily transform false claims into hyper realistic videos

From IDMO. Newsguard analysis finds Google's latest AI video generator integrated into Gemini can create highly realistic videos based on false or misleading claims with relatively simple prompts. While Google has introduced safeguards, Newsguard notes these are not always effective, underscoring the need for stronger detection technologies, media literacy, transparent content labelling, and responsible platform governance.

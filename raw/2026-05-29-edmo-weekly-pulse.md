## Overview

This edition explores the rise of AI-generated synthetic political influencers as a new weapon for extremist propaganda, focusing on the case of "Danny Bones" — a nonexistent far-right rapper created by The Node Project and funded by Advance UK. The ZOOM-IN section examines the broader transformation of political communications through synthetic content, growing American anger at AI, and Meta's investigation of an AI video impersonating Hungarian opposition figure Péter Magyar. ELECTION BEAT covers Malta's general election and Cyprus's "Sandygate" scandal. GLOBAL PULSE examines flotilla misrepresentation claims, Ebola misinformation in DR Congo, and Russian disinformation about Ukraine drone attacks. ON A DIFFERENT NOTE highlights a Carnegie report on AI and democracy.

## ON THE RISE — AI-Political Influencers: the New Gods of Propaganda and Disinformation?

A crucial check on fanaticism has always been the simple fact that its prophets rarely manage to live up to their own public sermons. Artificial Intelligence may have handed extremists a new weapon: synthetic influencers.

The key case is "Danny Bones", a far-right, anti-immigration AI-generated rapper in Britain, created by The Node Project and funded by extremist group Advance UK. He was featured at the far-right "Unite the Kingdom" rally in London on May 16. Some followers are unaware of the deception; others do not care.

AI offers a way around the problem of moral hypocrisy: Danny Bones will never be caught taking bribes, never stumble out of a nightclub, never make off-colour remarks, never mellow with age. He is perfectly aligned with his message. He can be everywhere at once, hold thousands of personalised conversations simultaneously, seducing, persuading and radicalising at industrial scale. The phenomenon is still in its infancy.

## ZOOM-IN

Synthetic political influencers — flawless and infallible — duplicate human knowledge, memories, and needs, peddling propaganda on social media. Several investigations show the harmful dimensions and societal frustration with both the technology and the tech industry's unwillingness to mitigate risks.

**How Synthetic Content Transforms Political Communications** (EDMO/Facta) — Far-right movements across Europe increasingly use AI-generated images and videos. Campaigns documented in Germany (AfD), France, Poland, Italy, Ireland, Spain. GADMO uncovered a large network of AI-generated female personas promoting AfD on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook.

**Fear, Boos and Firebombs: AI Creates Growing Anger Among Americans** (NORDIS) — Growing US public dissatisfaction with AI expansion: job insecurity, rising energy consumption, environmental concerns. Constant exposure to AI-generated inflammatory material reduces trust in media, institutions, and fellow citizens.

**Meta Oversight Board is Investigating AI Video Impersonating Péter Magyar** (HDMO) — Pro-government influencer posted AI-generated Facebook video of synthetic Magyar reacting angrily to political controversy. Meta decided not to remove or label it as AI-generated. The Oversight Board launched public consultation on deepfakes and political content.

## ELECTION BEAT

**Malta General Election 2026 — Weekly Brief #3: Unearthing a Pandora's Box** (MEDDMO) — In final week before Malta's election: PM Robert Abela's misleading claim about cancer medication coverage; Polymarket prediction markets despite illegality; PN's AI chatbot generated inaccurate visuals; suspicious "troll farming" activity.

**Sandygate: The "True" Crime Case That Shook Up the Election Campaign and Public Debate in Cyprus** (MEDDMO) — Allegations by journalist Makarios Drousiotis accusing senior figures of corruption centred on a woman referred to as "Sandy." Cypriot media found inconsistencies: images traced to unrelated websites, audio clips likely from documentaries. Case influenced party positioning and debate on institutional trust.

## GLOBAL PULSE

**Flotilla Goals Misrepresented: Fact Checks Challenge Meloni's Claims and Highlight Legal Dispute** (IDMO/DE FACTO) — Meloni's claim that Global Sumud Flotilla falsely presented itself as humanitarian was misleading; organisers were transparent about dual humanitarian and protest goals. Israel's interception in international waters raises legal concerns.

**Misleading Ebola Claims Spread as Conflict and Misinformation Fuel Deadly Outbreak in DR Congo** (IBERIFIER/Newtral) — Viral claims about "Ebola reaching the United States" were misleading: US doctor infected in DRC evacuated to Berlin. Bundibugyo strain outbreak in eastern DRC with hundreds of suspected cases. Misinformation worsens situation — communities attributing disease to curses or denying its existence.

**False Claims About Ukraine Drone Attacks From Latvia Debunked as Russian Disinformation** (BECID) — Russian officials claimed Ukraine preparing drone attacks from Latvian territory. Latvia, NATO, and Ukraine strongly deny. Part of broader Russian propaganda portraying NATO as aggressor.

## ON A DIFFERENT NOTE

**AI and Democracy: Mapping the Intersections** (Carnegie report) — AI influences democracy through multiple entry points: elections, citizen deliberation, government services, social cohesion. All face threats related to influence, integrity, and bias, but also present opportunities for targeted interventions.

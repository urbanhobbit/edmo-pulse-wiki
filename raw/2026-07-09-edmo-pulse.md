## Sections

### ON THE RISE
Europe Caught in the Crossfire of NATO-related Disinformation

The NATO summit in Ankara (7-8 July) takes place amid unprecedented uncertainty about the Alliance's future, with both the US (under Trump) and Russia spreading disinformation that weakens NATO. The US spreads narratives about NATO expenditure to pressure allies, while Russia pushes three main narratives: NATO expansion justifies aggression against Ukraine, NATO is actively fighting in Ukraine, and NATO involvement risks WWIII escalation.

### ZOOM-IN
- **Ukraine does not attack allies with drones** (BECID): Pro-Kremlin accounts exploit drone incidents to falsely claim Ukraine is intentionally attacking NATO allies.
- **No, the BBC did not reveal "70,000 French mercenaries"** (EDMO BELUX): Viral claim debunked — forged video using Le Point branding originated from Russian Telegram channels.
- **Is support for Ukraine undermining social housing investments in Germany?** (GADMO): Misleading claim that €233M for Ukraine is solely for housing; only €25M of the total is for social housing.

### ELECTION BEAT
- **AI Video Invents Verdict on Fabricated Election Law** (GADMO): TikTok video falsely claims German Constitutional Court ruled on a non-existent electoral law reform.
- **Romanian Governmental Crisis Amplified on Facebook** (BROD): Coordinated amplification involving 402 accounts generating 73,987 coordination events during Romania's governmental crisis.

### GLOBAL PULSE
- **Russia deflects blame for Pechory Lavra attack** (BECID): After Russian strike on UNESCO site, pro-Kremlin sources claim Ukraine attacked its own heritage using AI imagery.
- **No, the KGB did not create the Palestinian flag** (ADMO): Flag design traces to 1916 Arab Revolt, predating Soviet involvement.
- **RT finds way back to X despite EU sanctions** (IDMO): @RT_on_X account gets 6M views in 5 days, raising questions about platform enforcement.

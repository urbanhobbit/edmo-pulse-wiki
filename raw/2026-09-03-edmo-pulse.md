## They're (not) eating the cats, again

**September 3, 2026** — EDMO Weekly Pulse

Anti-migrant pet-eating hoax resurfaces after the Ceuta crisis: 'They're (not) eating the cats, again' examines the viral false claim that migrants abuse animals — a classic dehumanisation strategy with Trump's 2024 Haiti precedent, now targeting Moroccan migrants (previously framed as 'zombies'). AI-generated outrage: Chequeado maps fake migrants, protests and voters spreading transnationally (London AI 'migrant neighbourhoods', a fabricated Muslim mayoral candidate in Germany, Mexico AI images of Venezuelan migrants looting). Fact-checks: Canada church fire caused by lightning, not arson by a Muslim migrant (AFP); UK has not agreed to accommodate '4,000 Moroccans' from Ceuta (The Journal); Danish asylum figures in a Ceuta-triggered border debate. Sweden's September 13 election: Russian 'Operation Overload' impersonating news outlets (Kristersson/Ebba Busch pedophile-ring claims) and local Sweden Democrats voter-fraud posts. Germany's Saxony-Anhalt state election: AfD at ~42%, coordinated operations (Storm-1516, Doppelgänger, Matryoshka) amid Berlin's tougher line on Russia (Russian House closure). EDMO Ireland: platforms ignored ~90% of non-AI disinformation in the 2025 presidential election. Nepal glacier-flood disaster videos. 'Hanover Institute' fake think tank poisoning AI chatbots on Israel/Gaza. Iceland's 'sea barons' and the EU referendum.

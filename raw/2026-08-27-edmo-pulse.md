## Ceuta Crisis: First fuelled, then exploited by disinformation

**August 27, 2026** — EDMO Weekly Pulse

Ceuta migration crisis fuelled and exploited by disinformation. Maldita.es investigation: Arabic-language disinformation triggered the mass influx of 70,000+ people into the Spanish exclave.

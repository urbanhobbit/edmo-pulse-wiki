## Rewriting the past, endangering the future

**September 10, 2026** — EDMO Weekly Pulse

Historical memory as a weapon: Ratko Mladić's death reignites one of the Balkans' most toxic battles, with Serbian politicians, media and public figures celebrating the convicted génocidaire as a hero and denying Srebrenica, amplified by Russian state media — an aggressive paranoid nationalism that pushes Serbia away from the EU and raises the risk of renewed violence. Germany's AfD, fresh from 44% in Saxony-Anhalt, proposes cutting Holocaust and Nazism teaching and abolishing the civic-education agency; a redacted German memory serves both Moscow and Washington, which now root for AfD to dismantle the EU. ZOOM-IN digs into historical revisionism online: Maldita/Facta's 2025 'pop fascism' investigation (Franco, Mussolini and Hitler repackaged as memes, music, AI videos and 'Führer Fridays' on TikTok, X, YouTube, Telegram, normalising fascist imagery among young audiences); Provereno Media's 'Holocaust for Sale' exposé of AI-generated fake Holocaust victims on monetised Facebook pages (Auschwitz-Birkenau Museum calls it 'falsification of history'; EDMO Belux's media-literacy tool 'The illusion of the archive'); Funky Citizens/BROD analysis of 9,000+ Telegram posts from 35 groups in Romania's 2024 presidential campaign (Călin Georgescu's idealised Dacian heritage, praise for Legionary leader Codreanu and dictator Antonescu, Holocaust downplaying); and AfD's romanticised Bismarck-era Imperial Germany nostalgia replacing post-WWII remembrance. ELECTION BEAT: a Swedish technical error misregisters up to 500 early votes, fuelling 'election fraud' rumours and the false claim that Election Authority head Anna Nyqvist is a Social Democrat; a viral hospital-closure list omits a centre-right-closure case; and a false German claim that media suppressed the child-abuse sentence of a former SPD deputy mayor (widely covered by Zeit, Spiegel, WDR), countering AfD's 'public-broadcaster capture' narrative. GLOBAL PULSE: the New York Times did NOT report that Saudi Arabia threatened Israel with 'full-scale war' if Turkey were attacked (MedDMO/Cyprus University of Technology); Brazil's October 4 election pits Lula against Flávio Bolsonaro, with an Argentine network falsely claiming Lula triggered a Moroccan migrant influx (the story rested on 50 migrants helped in Florianópolis); and VoxCheck's fact-check of Putin's post-SCO-summit claims (battlefield advances near Sumy/Donetsk, the Black Sea Grain Initiative, strikes on civilian grain vessels, energy-infrastructure 'retaliation'). ON A DIFFERENT NOTE: a Council of Europe study by Iva Nenadić and Marijana Grbeša argues newsfluencers are ambivalent actors — simultaneously a source of democratic opportunity and democratic vulnerability.
